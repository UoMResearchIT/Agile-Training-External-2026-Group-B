# Chicken Tikka Masala (Serves 4)

## Ingredients
### For the marinade:

- 500g boneless chicken (thigh or breast), cut into bite-sized pieces
- 150g natural yoghurt
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp paprika or Kashmiri chilli powder
- ½ tsp turmeric
- Salt, to taste
- Juice of ½ lemon

## For the sauce:

- 2 tbsp vegetable oil or ghee
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp chilli powder (adjust to taste)
- 400g tin chopped tomatoes
- 100ml double cream
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- Salt, to taste
- Fresh coriander, chopped (for garnish)


## Method
### Step 1: Marinate the chicken

In a large bowl, combine the yoghurt, garlic, ginger, spices, salt and lemon juice.
Add the chicken pieces and coat well.
Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour (overnight is preferable for deeper flavour).


### Step 2: Cook the chicken

Preheat your grill to a high setting (or use a hot frying pan).
Thread the chicken onto skewers (optional) and grill for 10–15 minutes, turning occasionally, until slightly charred and cooked through.
Set aside.


### Step 3: Prepare the sauce

Heat the oil or ghee in a large pan over medium heat.
Add the chopped onion and cook gently for 8–10 minutes until softened and lightly golden.
Stir in the garlic and ginger, cooking for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.


### Step 4: Add spices and tomatoes

Add the cumin, coriander, garam masala and chilli powder. Cook for 1 minute.
Stir in the tomato purée, followed by the chopped tomatoes.
Simmer gently for 10–15 minutes until slightly thickened.


### Step 5: Finish the dish

Add the grilled chicken pieces to the sauce.
Stir in the cream and season with salt to taste.
Simmer for a further 5–10 minutes until the chicken is fully coated and the sauce is rich and smooth.


## To Serve

Garnish with freshly chopped coriander.
Serve with steamed basmati rice, naan bread or chapatis.


## Tips

For a smoky flavour, you can finish the chicken under a very hot grill briefly before adding it to the sauce.
If you prefer a lighter dish, substitute the double cream with single cream or coconut milk.
Adjust chilli levels to suit your taste.
