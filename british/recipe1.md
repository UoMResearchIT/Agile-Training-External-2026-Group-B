# Lancashire Hotpot

## Serves
4

## Ingredients
- 700g lamb neck or shoulder, cut into chunks
- 2 tbsp plain flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil or dripping
- 2 onions, sliced
- 2 carrots, sliced (optional)
- 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- 500ml lamb or beef stock
- 2 bay leaves
- 900g potatoes, peeled and thinly sliced
- 25g butter, melted

## Method
1. Preheat the oven to **160°C fan / 180°C / gas 4**.
2. Toss the lamb with flour, salt, and pepper.
3. Heat oil in a heavy pan and brown the lamb in batches. Set aside.
4. In the same pan, cook onions (and carrots if using) for 5 minutes until softened.
5. Return lamb to the pan. Add Worcestershire sauce, stock, and bay leaves. Bring to a simmer for 5 minutes.
6. Transfer to an ovenproof casserole dish.
7. Layer potato slices over the top, slightly overlapping. Brush with melted butter.
8. Cover with a lid (or foil) and bake for **1 hour 30 minutes**.
9. Remove lid/foil, raise oven to **200°C fan / 220°C / gas 7**, and cook another **30 minutes** until potatoes are golden and crisp.
10. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

## Serving Suggestion
Serve with red cabbage, peas, or crusty bread.
