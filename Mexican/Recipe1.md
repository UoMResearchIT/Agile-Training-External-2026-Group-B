# Chicken Enchiladas Verdes

## Cuisine
Mexican

### Times
- Prep: 20 minutes
- Cook: 30 minutes
- Total: 50 minutes

### Ingredients
- 2 lbs chicken breast
- 8 corn tortillas
- 2 cups green salsa (salsa verde)
- 1 cup sour cream
- 2 cups shredded Oaxaca cheese
- 1/2 white onion, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Oil for cooking

### Method
1. Boil chicken with onion and salt until cooked through (about 20 minutes). Shred the chicken.
2. Mix green salsa with sour cream in a bowl.
3. Heat oil in a pan and warm each tortilla briefly to soften.
4. Dip tortilla in salsa mixture, fill with shredded chicken and cheese, then roll and place seam-side down in a greased baking dish.
5. Pour remaining salsa mixture over enchiladas and top with remaining cheese.
6. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 25-30 minutes until bubbly and golden.
7. Serve hot with fresh cilantro and lime wedges.

### Other Notes
- Can be prepared ahead and refrigerated before baking
- For a richer sauce, add 1/2 cup Mexican crema
- Serve with Mexican rice and black beans on the side
